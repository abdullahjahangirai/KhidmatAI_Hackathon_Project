ABOUT KHIDMATAI

An AI-powered welfare navigation platform for Pakistan.

KhidmatAI helps people discover verified welfare programs, scholarships, healthcare support, financial assistance and nearby services — all in one simple experience. Support exists across Pakistan; our mission is to make finding it clear, simple and fast.
